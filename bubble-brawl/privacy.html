Bubble Brawl — Privacy Policy

Bubble Brawl is a game made by Akinware (Akin Akcam, Forstweg 16, 98646 Hildburghausen, Germany · akinakcam@akinware.live). This policy explains what data the game uses. Last updated: October 2026.

Data stored on your device

Your records (enemies popped, worlds cleared, biggest tank) and your sound setting are saved only on your device. We do not run servers, accounts or analytics, and we do not collect your name, e-mail or location.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): an optional rewarded ad that lets you continue a run, and an occasional full-screen ad between worlds. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, and information about ad interactions to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy choices" on the title screen. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Bubble Brawl has no in-app purchases.

Children

Bubble Brawl is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your Bubble Brawl data

Bubble Brawl has no accounts, so there is nothing for you to sign in to or close. To delete your data:

  1. Game records: uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → Bubble Brawl → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your records immediately and completely; we keep no copy.
  2. Advertising data: open Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) and choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID". You can also change your ad choices on the game's title screen.
  3. Anything else: e-mail akinakcam@akinware.live with the subject "Bubble Brawl data deletion". We answer within 30 days. We hold no personal data ourselves, so we will tell you how to reach Google for data that Google processes.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict processing of your data and to object to processing. As we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google. You may also complain to a data protection authority. Contact: akinakcam@akinware.live.
