Potion Heroes — Privacy Policy

Potion Heroes is a game made by Akinware (Akin Akcam, Forstweg 16, 98646 Hildburghausen, Germany · akinakcam@akinware.live). This policy explains what data the game uses. Last updated: October 2026.

Your game progress

Your progress (gold, gems, heroes, gear, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run a server and do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch them for a reward) and occasional full-screen ads between chapters. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and can change their choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Purchases are recorded on your device so the game can grant them.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your score is submitted to the game's leaderboard under your Play Games profile. This is optional.

Children

Potion Heroes is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your Potion Heroes data

Potion Heroes has no accounts, so there is nothing for you to sign in to or close. To delete your data:

  1. Game progress: uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → Potion Heroes → Storage → Clear data. In the web version, clear the site data for this website in your browser. This deletes your progress immediately and completely; we keep no copy.
  2. Advertising data: open Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) and choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID". You can also change your ad choices in the game's settings.
  3. Leaderboard score: open the Google Play Games app → Profile → Settings → "Delete Play Games account & data", or remove Potion Heroes from your Play Games account.
  4. Anything else: e-mail akinakcam@akinware.live with the subject "Potion Heroes data deletion". We answer within 30 days. We hold no personal data ourselves, so we will tell you how to reach Google for data that Google processes.
