01 · Games

Eight games, coming soon to Google Play

Every game here is finished, tested and getting ready for the Google Play Store. Each one has its own look and its own reason to play one more round.

Google Play Store · coming soonPlayable in your browser now

Relic RushOutrun the tiger. Grab the gold.

3D endless runner

Race through ancient jungle ruins with a tiger on your heels. Swipe to switch lanes, jump, slide and turn at crossroads while the run gets faster and faster.

  • Four zones, from sunrise ruins to a moonlit temple, plus zipline and mine-cart sections
  • 14 explorers with their own perks, daily missions and the RELIC letter hunt
  • A charged power for every run, relic chests and boosters like Head Start and Gold Rush
Play in your browser → Play on itch.io
Google Play Store · coming soon

Rail RascalsDodge trains. Grab coins. Rush!

3D endless runner

Dash through a graffiti-covered rail yard with the yard keeper on your tail. Switch tracks, jump barriers, roll under signs and run across the roofs of moving trains.

  • Four districts that change as you go, from Downtown Yard to Neon Night
  • Fill the Rush meter, then smash straight through everything in your way
  • Jetpacks, coin magnets, spring shoes and 2× score, all upgradable
  • 8 rascals and 5 hoverboards to unlock, daily missions and a word hunt
Google Play Store · coming soon

Peak PlungeCarve the ridge. Outrun the avalanche.

Endless downhill ski run

Drag to carve down the mountain between pines and boulders. Thread the slalom gates, flip off ramps and keep your speed up, because the avalanche never stops.

  • Four landscapes on the way down: Pine Forest, Alpine Village, Frozen Lake and Aurora Night
  • Flips and gate combos multiply your score
  • Rocket skis, coin magnet, helmet shield and double coins
  • 8 skiers and snowboarders to unlock
Google Play Store · coming soon

Gate ArmyShoot the gates. Grow the squad.

Runner shooter

Steer your squad down the road and shoot the right gates to multiply your soldiers. Every level ends with a boss, so the bigger your army, the faster it falls.

  • Six worlds, from Desert Outpost to Moon Base, each with its own giant boss
  • Six weapons to unlock: Rifle, SMG, Shotgun, Minigun, Rocket and Laser
  • 10 uniforms, squad upgrades and a Boss Rush mode
  • Endless levels and new missions every day
Google Play Store · coming soon

ManafallPour. Multiply. Conquer.

Pixel-art auto-battler

Your hero fights wave after wave on their own, and every fallen enemy drops mana orbs. Between waves you pour the orbs through rune gates that multiply them, then spend the mana on new skills.

  • Five heroes, each with a signature skill: Arrow Rain, Overclock, Meteor, Bulwark and Shadow Step
  • Six realms of ten waves each, with a boss waiting at the end
  • Fire, ice and storm gates power up your hero, plus 22 skill cards to combine
  • Loot gear and merge three into one stronger item
Google Play Store · coming soon

WickboundLight the way down.

Party dungeon crawler

When the great Lantern of Emberfall goes out, Wick, the last living flame, leads a band of misfit heroes into the Underdeep. Your party fights on its own. You unleash their abilities, choose which door to take and keep Wick's oil burning before the dark closes in.

  • Six heroes with their own abilities: Cleave, Volley, Sanctuary, Firestorm, Shadowstep and Anthem
  • Five dungeons and five bosses, 20 chambers per delve
  • Shrines, oil springs, treasure and elite brutes behind every fork
  • Grow the Lantern Tree at camp and collect 17 relics
Google Play Store · coming soon

DrillheartDig deep. Get rich. Make it home.

Pixel-art mining adventure

Pilot a little drill pod with a glowing heart down through the earth below Hollow Creek. Every trip is a bet: fill your hold with ore, then fly back before the fuel runs out. Sell, upgrade and dive deeper.

  • Seven strata to dig through, from Topsoil and Crystal Caves to Magma Deep and the Heart Core
  • Thirteen ores, from coal and copper to diamonds, platinum and heartshards
  • Upgrade the drill, engine, fuel tank, cargo bay, hull, cooling and radar
  • Rebuild old waystations, find the founders' journal and reach the Heartstone 1200 m down
Google Play Store · coming soon

Lantern SortSort the fireflies. Light up the village.

Cozy sorting puzzle

Night has fallen over Firefly Hollow. Sort the glowing fireflies into jars by colour: every finished jar closes into a lantern and lights up the jars beside it. Coins from each level bring the sleepy village back to light, piece by piece.

  • 600 levels, each checked by a solver, with dark fireflies, sleepy fireflies and frozen jars
  • A new lantern reveals the hidden fireflies next to it, so the order you finish jars matters
  • Restore five village chapters, from Mosswood Lane to Starfall Grove
  • No timers, no lives: free undos, hints, a daily gift and a daily puzzle

An independent studio from Germany
