01 · Products

Two products, both live

Each one started as a prototype and was finished, tested and released. Both run in any modern browser, with no account and no sign-up.

Playable in the browser · Android app coming to Google Play

Relic RushOutrun the tiger. Grab the gold.

A 3D endless runner through ancient jungle ruins, with a tiger on your heels. Swipe to switch lanes, jump, slide and turn at crossroads while the run gets faster.

  • Four zones, from sunrise ruins to a moonlit temple, plus zipline and mine-cart sections
  • 14 explorers with their own perks, daily missions and the RELIC letter hunt
  • Built with Three.js; packaged as a native Android app for Google Play
  • Works offline after the first load. Progress stays on your device
Play in your browser → Privacy policy
Live · free to use

Job HunterFind jobs without ten open tabs.

One search across several job boards. Drop in your CV and every listing gets a match score, with the skills you have and the ones the job asks for.

  • Searches several job boards at once and removes duplicates
  • CV matching runs on your own device. Your CV is never uploaded
  • No account, no sign-up, no tracking
  • Germany and remote jobs today, more countries next

04 · About

An independent studio from Germany

Akinware was founded in 2026 by Akin Akcam, a developer who builds games, web tools and app prototypes. Everything here is designed, built and released in-house.

The idea behind the tools is simple: the information you need is already online, spread across many sites. Software should collect it, compare it and give you the short version. The games follow the same rule: easy to start, hard to put down.
