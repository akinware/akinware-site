02 · How we build

Small tools, built on a few firm rules

[ many → one ]

Every source, one answer

We ask many sources in parallel, merge what they return, remove duplicates and show the result in one place.

[ local ]

Your data stays yours

Anything personal, like a CV, is processed in your browser. We don't run accounts, ads or trackers.

[ cited ]

Always say where it's from

Every result links back to the original listing, and every source is named, so you can check it yourself.
