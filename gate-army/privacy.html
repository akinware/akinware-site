Gate Army — Privacy Policy

Gate Army is a game made by Akinware (Akin Akcam, Forstweg 16, 98646 Hildburghausen, Germany · akinakcam@akinware.live). This policy explains what data the game uses. Last updated: October 2026.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (level, coins, gems, upgrades, unlocked weapons and uniforms, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers, accounts or analytics, and we do not collect your name, e-mail or location.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, and information about ad interactions to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Android app)

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google tells the app only which item was bought so it can be unlocked. Google's privacy policy applies.

Leaderboard (Android app, optional)

The weekly Boss Rush leaderboard uses Google Play Games Services. Only when you open the leaderboard or sign in to Play Games does the app send your Boss Rush score to Google, which shows it with your Play Games profile name and avatar. Play Games is run by Google under its own privacy policy and settings: policies.google.com/privacy.

Web version

The browser version stores progress in your browser's local storage and works offline after the first visit (service worker cache). It shows no ads and offers no purchases. It is hosted by Vercel Inc., which processes technically necessary server log data (such as IP address and time of access) to deliver the site.

Children

Gate Army is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict processing of your data and to object to processing. As we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google. You may also complain to a data protection authority. Contact: akinakcam@akinware.live.
